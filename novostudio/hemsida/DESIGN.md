# DESIGN.md – Novo Studio Hemsida

## Designtokens & Färgpalett (Organic Tech)
- **Warm Cream (Bakgrund):** `#FAF8F5`
- **Ink Charcoal (Text):** `#2A221F`
- **Deep Moss (Primär accent & knappar):** `#2E4036`
- **Clay Terracotta (Sekundär accent):** `#CC5833`
- **Emerald Green (Status & SSL):** `#059669`
- **Hairline Border:** `#D4CFC6`

## Typografi
- **Display Headings:** Cormorant Garamond (`font-cormorant`, 400 italic)
- **Section Headings:** Fraunces (`font-soft-serif`, 300/400)
- **Body Text:** Outfit (`font-body-tight`, 300/400)
- **Data & Metric Metrics:** IBM Plex Mono (`font-data-mono`, 400)

## Hörnradier & Skuggor
- **Kort / Paneler:** `rounded-[40px]` eller `rounded-xl` med `1px solid #D4CFC6` eller `border-ink/10`.
- **Taktila knappar:** `shadow-[0_2px_0_0_#1a241e]`, hover `-translate-y-0.5`.
